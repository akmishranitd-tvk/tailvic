# Approved grooming tariffs

Status: implementing
Owner or requester: Tailvic owner
Last updated: 2026-11-26
Related issue or request: Approved tariff implementation in Codex

## Goal

Publish one accurate, maintainable source of customer prices for the existing
grooming service and use it consistently across the site.

## Scope

Use the approved dog size/package and cat coat/package prices on homepage cards,
service and package pages, and the booking estimator where that application
route is available. Store new booking estimates as snapshots; never recalculate
existing booking quotes when tariffs change. Express Care remains custom quoted.
Do not add discounts, taxes, service inclusions, or backend availability claims.

## Acceptance criteria

- All 16 dog and 6 cat package combinations match the approved tariff.
- Dog estimates use size; cat estimates use short or long coat.
- A custom Express Care price is not invented.
- Customer prices come from one central configuration; legacy service price
  fields are removed from service records.
- Published pricing tables remain readable on narrow screens through horizontal
  scrolling, and the site keeps its existing desktop layout.
- A booking retains the amount saved when it was created or quoted.
- The site remains noindex and existing static booking limitations remain clear.

## Technical approach

`pricing.py` owns the tariff. Templates and the booking estimator consume that
configuration through `app.py`; static pages are regenerated by the existing
exporter. Historic booking quote columns remain separate.

## Open decisions

The approved tariff request did not provide inclusions for Hygiene Groom,
Signature Coat Spa, or the three cat package tiers. These are identified as
scope confirmed with Tailvic before a visit; no unapproved inclusions are
claimed. Approved inclusions for Bath & Brush and Full Style Groom are retained.
